CoStudy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

CoStudy ("we", "us"), operated by the developer of costudy.in, provides an AI-powered study platform for CMA US candidates, accessible via our website and Android application. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name and email address when you sign up (authentication is handled by Supabase).
  • User content: posts you publish to the study wall, doubts you ask, messages, mock-test results, and profile information you choose to add.
  • Usage data: basic app interaction data needed to operate features such as study rooms and progress tracking.

How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To provide the app's core features (study wall, rooms, mock tests, AI tools, messaging).
  • To respond to support requests and doubt submissions.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show third-party ads.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure and transmitted over HTTPS. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect it, but no online service is completely secure.

Data retention and deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing midhunnr@gmail.com. We will delete your account data within 30 days of a verified request, except where retention is required by law.

Children's privacy

CoStudy is intended for exam candidates and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this page.

Contact

Questions or data requests: midhunnr@gmail.com
